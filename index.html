About

I run product functions end to end. Nine years in fintech and B2B SaaS, most recently as Head of Product at Backflip, a venture-backed real estate lender, where I was the only product person: I owned the technology roadmap for the engineering team, ran the cross-functional pods, and built the AI systems I ran product on.

Before that, six years at Kasasa in banking SaaS, moving from Associate PM to Senior PM. I owned one of the company’s core consumer banking products end to end, launched four variations of it that reached $1.2M in annual revenue, and led the migration of 600 client banks and credit unions onto a new platform, with tooling that cut support cost by $250K. Before product, I ran a 90-person shift at an Amazon fulfillment center.

I run research, analysis, documentation, and prototyping on agents I built, every day, for two years now. The practical effect is that I hold more of the product at once and get to a first draft in hours, so the time I spend with engineers, sales, and borrowers goes to decisions rather than status.

None of that is why I do this, though. I do product because I like building things that solve a real problem and seeing people actually want to use them. What sets a good PM apart, in my opinion, is curiosity: you have to be genuinely curious about the world to understand your customer, to keep learning and adapting, and to be fine with being wrong. That is my default setting, and it has made me a better product leader. I am looking for like-minded people, products that solve real problems, and a company that is excited to embrace AI to serve its customers better.
