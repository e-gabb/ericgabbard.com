About

I’m a Head of Product with nine years in fintech and B2B SaaS. Most recently at Backflip, a real estate lending platform, where I was the only product person — I owned the full technology roadmap for our engineering team and built the AI systems I ran product on.

Before that, six and a half years at Kasasa in banking SaaS, moving from Associate PM to Senior PM. I owned one of the company’s core consumer banking products end to end and led the migration of the client base onto a new platform. Before product, I ran a 90-person shift at an Amazon fulfillment center.

What separates my work now is how far I’ve gone with AI. I’ve pushed on it further than most product leaders I’ve worked with, and not as an experiment — it’s how I run research, analysis, product documentation, and prototyping every day. That habit compounds: I move faster, I hold more of the product at once, and I understand what my teams are building at a depth I couldn’t reach when every answer had to come off someone else’s calendar.

It also keeps me honest about where AI actually helps. Most teams reach for automation when the real problem is that their data can’t be reasoned over. The two look identical from the outside, and only one of them holds up as you build on it.
