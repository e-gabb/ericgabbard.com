Selected work

Three problems worth the space. Each one starts with a number somebody disagreed with.

Making retention the roadmap

Rebuilt the roadmap around new-user retention, then ran a cross-functional pod through 10+ funnel experiments — and showed sales why retention was their problem too.

Saving the product everyone wanted to cut

A feature was on the chopping block for its data bill. The data said it was the single strongest retention driver we had. I kept the product and killed the bill.

Rebuilding a live A/B test that was lying to us

Three variants, two opposed stakeholder agendas, and an attribution pipeline quietly discarding three quarters of real mobile traffic. Fixed mid-flight.

Also

  • Loan dashboard architecture. Greenlit a React rewrite and cut the database middle layer in favor of a direct CRM front end — median time to close fell 27% (26 days to 19), engineering feature velocity roughly tripled.
  • Data warehouse & securitization reporting. Locked scope at the raw-source and per-loan-derived layers to avoid blocking on downstream logic, inventoried 21 reports across credit, capital markets, accounting and reconciliation, and reduced 500–600 apparent CRM fields to roughly 110 unique source fields.
  • Regulatory risk in the funnel. Worked with compliance on denial-of-credit exposure by redefining the application trigger point — an “application” to “loan quote” terminology shift that preserved the funnel and avoided an automated filtering system that would have disqualified good leads.
  • Vendor migration off a legacy draw-management platform. Risk-tiered by a 50% loan-completion threshold, with a hard trigger condition that bounded the dual-system period instead of leaving the cutover open-ended.
